PacketBridge v0.1 — read-me first

WHAT THIS IS
PacketBridge reads a TWC assistive-technology referral packet (VR1884 + Service Authorization + EAS Report, all PDFs) and shows you a plain-language readout: what was found, what training is being requested, any conflicts between the documents, and what is still missing.

Everything runs on THIS computer, in your browser, offline. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. There is no account and no tracking.

HOW TO OPEN IT
1. Unzip this folder somewhere you will keep it (for example, your Desktop or Documents).
2. Open the PacketBridge folder.
3. Right-click index.html and choose "Open with" -> Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
   (Or drag index.html into an open Chrome/Edge window.)
4. It works with no internet connection. Try it: turn Wi-Fi off and it still works.

HOW TO USE V0.1
1. Under "1. Load the referral packet", pick the three PDFs:
   - VR1884 Referral (required — it must be the fillable PDF, not a scanned/flattened copy)
   - Service Authorization (recommended)
   - EAS Consultation Report (recommended)
2. Press "Read packet".
3. Read the readout under "2. Readout". Screen-reader users: after pressing Read, focus moves to the Readout heading; navigate by headings from there.

WHAT V0.1 DOES NOT DO YET
- It does not generate the VR2868 / VR2902 forms yet. That is the next build step, along with the report-details form (provider profile, sessions, ratings).
- Packets in Word (.docx) format are not read yet — PDF only for now.
- Scanned paper packets are not read (no OCR).

PRIVACY NOTE FOR REAL PACKETS
Only use fictional/training packets until your instructor or agency says otherwise. When you do use it: the files never leave your machine, but the filled forms you later generate will be normal PDFs — handle them with the same care (and encrypted email) as any TWC paperwork.

Forms: VR2868 rev 08/2025, VR2902 rev 06/2022, verified against the TWC website 2026-10-28.
