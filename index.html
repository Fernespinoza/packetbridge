4. Generate the report packet

Builds the three filled PDFs from the packet you read and the details you entered, in packet order: VR2902 Baseline, VR2868, VR2902 Post-Training. In Prep mode the assessment ratings and scores stay blank and unassessed subjects are marked N/A, matching the course shells. In Complete mode your ratings and their automatic scores are filled in. Signatures are always left blank — those are signed by hand. Before filling, each template's fingerprint is checked; if TWC has revised a form, generation stops with a warning instead of filling the wrong revision. The PDFs are generated on this computer and download straight to it — nothing is uploaded.
